dart2js compile time vs. number of classes overriding ==

2026-10-02T04:30:27.554319 · Dart 3.13.5 · macos Version 27.0.1 (Build 26A434) · 10 cores · 17 compiles, N = 1 to 7000

Each generated class has an int field, a hashCode override and a toString. The variants differ only in their operator ==: its parameter type, or no override at all. Each point is a single compile.

Breakdown for == (dynamic other)

  • Compile time went from 376ms at N=1 to 40.8s at N=7000, 108× slower for 7000× more classes. From N=1750 up it scales as roughly N1.81 (about quadratic).
  • Most of the extra time comes from Enqueue (+25.7s, 64% of the increase), SSA builder (+5.86s, 15% of the increase) and SSA optimizer (+3.28s, 8.1% of the increase).
  • Fastest-growing phases that take at least 5% of the time at N=7000: Enqueue > resolution.dynamicUse (N2.47, 62% of the time), Enqueue (N2.39, 63% of the time) and SSA builder (N1.70, 14% of the time). These grow faster than the total, so they will dominate even more at larger N.
  • Between N=100 and N=7000, Enqueue grew from 4.8% to 63% of compile time, while kernel loader fell from 36% to 3.1%.
  • The amount of work dart2js reports grows at most as N0.98 (about 40 inferred types per class), but time grows as N1.81. The cost per element is rising with program size, which points at non-linear behavior inside the phases above rather than just more code to compile.
  • Outside the compiler itself (VM startup, make), each compile costs a roughly fixed 95ms of wall-clock time on average.
